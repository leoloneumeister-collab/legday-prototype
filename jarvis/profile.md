# Investor profile

This file is the part of Jarvis you tune. It is injected verbatim into the system
prompt every run. Edit it whenever the brief flags the wrong things — that is the
intended feedback loop, not a code change.

## Who I am

- Individual investor, long-only, Trading212 Invest account, based in Europe.
- Small account. A €50 move is not news; a thesis breaking is.
- I check this once in the morning. I am not day-trading and I do not want to be.

## Thesis

- Core bet: AI / tech momentum — compute demand, the semiconductor supply chain,
  and the hyperscalers spending into it.
- I hold positions for months, not days. I add to winners rather than trade around
  them.

## Risk tolerance

- Comfortable with volatility in individual names; not comfortable with a
  concentrated position quietly becoming half the book.
- Things I actually want to hear about: a holding's thesis being contradicted by
  news, position concentration drifting, a regulatory or export-control change that
  hits the AI supply chain, and an earnings date I have not noticed.

## Bar for telling me something

Stay silent unless it clears this bar. Specifically:

- **Do not** report normal daily price movement as if it were information.
- **Do not** relay headlines that merely mention a company I hold.
- **Do** flag it when news changes what a holding is worth owning, when I have
  drifted from my own rules, or when something needs a decision from me today.
- If nothing clears the bar, say so in one line. A quiet day is a valid answer and
  I would rather read "nothing needs you" than filler.
