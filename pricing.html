Questions

Does the free tier expire?
No. Logging and the streak are free permanently. Paid tiers are for planning and coaching on top.
Can I change tier later?
Any month, in either direction. Changes take effect at the next billing date and nothing is deleted.
Is there a family or crew price?
Pro covers up to four people in one household at the single price.
What happens to my data?
It stays yours. Export the full history as CSV at any tier, including Free.
